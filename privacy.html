Privacy Policy

Gage Gossman · Effective November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gage Gossman ("we", "us"), a sole proprietor operating a missed-call text assistant, collects and uses information when you call a business phone number served by our assistant and exchange text messages with it.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number, when you call a number served by our assistant.
  • Voicemail recordings and their transcripts, if you leave a message.
  • The content of text messages you send and receive in the conversation.
  • Details you choose to share about your service request, such as your first name, ZIP code or address, a description of the problem, and preferred appointment times.

How we use your information

We use this information only to respond to your call, understand your service request, schedule an appointment, and pass your request to the business you called. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We share information only with the business you called, so they can respond to your request, and with service providers that operate the assistant on our behalf, such as our telecommunications provider (to deliver calls and texts) and an AI service provider (to help draft replies). These providers may use the information only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text message opt-out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. After you opt out, we will not text you again unless you reply START.

Data retention and security

We keep conversation records, voicemail recordings and transcripts for up to 90 days, unless the business you called needs them longer to complete your service request. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with a new effective date.
