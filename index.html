Text messaging terms

When you call (812) 558-2346 and we miss your call, you'll hear a short greeting, and then you'll receive a text message from Gage Gossman's missed-call assistant about your call. We only text the number that called. If you reply, the assistant will ask about your request and help schedule a time.

  • Message frequency varies. You get one text after a missed call, and more only while you're replying.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • We never sell or share your information for marketing.
